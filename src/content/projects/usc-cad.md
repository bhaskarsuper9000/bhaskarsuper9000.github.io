As a student research assistant (DARPA) at the Spatial Sciences Institute, USC, from September 2016 to June 2017, I conceptualised and built an interoperable CAD format with embedded semantics. The paper, "Automatic learning of user design rationales from examples", was presented at the Symposium on Solid and Physical Modeling in Berkeley, June 2017.
