My MTech project: mapping defects on a road surface in real time with a Kinect, the Point Cloud Library and machine learning. The aims were a more accurate road-mapping system, a sensor chosen for cost against accuracy, a relative GPS station, machine-learning classification and validation of the collected data, and TIN generation.
