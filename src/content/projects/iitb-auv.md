Software team member on Matsya, IIT Bombay's student-built 6 DOF autonomous underwater vehicle (auv-iitb.org), from September 2013 to October 2014. I ported drivers, tested navigation, added mapping and integrated the software with the vision subsystem. I also worked on localisation and tracking algorithms, extended the map-based visualisation tool, and helped simulate and test the software stack.
