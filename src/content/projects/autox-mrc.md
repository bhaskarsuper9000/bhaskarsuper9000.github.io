I led a team of 4 on the Minimum Risk Condition (MRC) project: what the vehicle does gracefully when something fails. The work included an AV safety subsystem with an API for other teams, and collaboration with them on recovery from crashes (C++, ISO 26262, crash diagnostics). It handles 93% of the failure cases.
